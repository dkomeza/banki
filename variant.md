# d: Grip sheet

b's sheet, opened from "Ask about this" under the Feedback (a's place for it), at any size the User drags it to, and hidden into a bar that keeps the chat on screen. Below 1024 px the sheet rises from the dock with a grip on its top edge: drag it up or down, or tap it to jump to its tallest and back. From 1024 px it is a side sheet at the window's right edge whose left edge drags it wider or narrower (double-click for the default width), and the Card makes room to match. Arrow keys resize from a focused grip, and the size is kept across reloads. Hide (the chevron in its header, a tap above it on a phone, or "Hide chat" under the Feedback) folds the sheet into a bar on the dock below 1024 px and a rail at the right edge from there. The bar shows the message count, or "Replying…" while a reply streams on behind it, and a tap brings the sheet back as it was. Under the Feedback the button reads "Show chat" with the count once the chat has started.

States: graded, chat-empty, chat, chat-streaming, chat-failed, chat-cut-off, chat-long, chat-hidden

Routes: `/decks/5eed0000-0000-4000-8000-000000000002/study` (Calculus 2, write-and-grade). The plain load is the Queue's first Card: write an answer and Check, and the stubbed Grader grades it after about 2 s; then ask away, and a canned reply streams in (Stop ends it). The state pill at the bottom steps through the states: `graded` is the ln(1 + x) sketch Card, the `chat-*` states the Maclaurin series Card, as in BAN-40's `graded-mistakes`. `chat-hidden` is `chat` with the sheet hidden (not in the Prototype section's list; it shows the hidden bar). Nothing reaches OpenRouter.

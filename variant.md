# g: Margin, in step

Builds on d (Margin). From 1024 px it is d's margin: each mistake's note level with its mistake, and the Feedback beside the Back, now held in view (sticky) while a long Back scrolls past. Below 1024 px, where d put everything after the Back, each note drops in under the paragraph of its mistake, inside your answer, and the Feedback comes straight after your answer and before the Back, its place held from Check. Missing parts are one even tint with a solid underline, instead of d's dashed, broken-up marks. The dock is d's: the suggestion filled in the accent, a "Confirm Hard" callout over a selection.

States: grading, grading-selected, graded, graded-mistakes, grade-failed, no-key

Routes: `/decks/5eed0000-0000-4000-8000-000000000002/study` (Calculus 2, write-and-grade). The plain load is the first Card's Front; write an answer and Check, and the stubbed Grader answers after about 2.5 s with a canned grade. The state pill at the bottom steps through the states. `graded-mistakes` shows the Maclaurin series Card in place of the first one (Edit opens the real one).

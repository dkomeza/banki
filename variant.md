# k: Zoom only

j, and the Front has no scale of its own: only the board's zoom makes it larger or smaller, together with the ink.

Built on h, for the other reading of the Maintainer's round-3 feedback ("remove the scale & fold away buttons from the front"): the Front has no menu, no Fold away and no "Bring the Front here", as in j, and no corner handles either. Tap it to select it (an accent frame), drag anywhere on it to move it, tap the paper to let go of it. A long Front still starts drawn to fit 60% of the canvas.

The board zooms, from 30% to 300%: two fingers pinch, Ctrl+wheel zooms about the pointer, and the control at the bottom-left has − / +, the zoom (tap it for 100%), and Fit, which shows the Front and all the ink at once, and finds the Front after panning it out of sight. One finger or the wheel pans. Keyboard: Tab to the Front, arrows move it.

States: ink, ink-written, ink-long-front

Routes: `/decks/5eed0000-0000-4000-8000-000000000002/study` (Calculus 2, write-and-grade). The state pill steps through the states; Handwrite in the top bar (`?handwriting=1`) puts whatever Card shows into handwriting. The ink is rough prototype ink: a pen or a mouse writes.

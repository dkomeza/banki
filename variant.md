# a: Ruled

Everything the editor adds stays on the page's ruled lines, in ink: a quiet row of icons on the line under the side being written (docked above the keyboard on a phone), the inline Formula's render on a slip of paper under it, a display Formula's render on an orange rule under its source, and the table as a hairline grid with its ⋯ just above its right edge.

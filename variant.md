# f: Float sheet

d's grip sheet, floating over the page at every size, so the Card never moves or narrows for it. Below 1024 px it rises from the dock as in d, with the grip on its top edge (drag it, or tap it to jump to its tallest and back). From 1024 px it is a card floating at the window's bottom right, just above the dock, over the Feedback margin: drag its left edge for width, its top edge for height, or the corner between them for both (double-click for the default; arrow keys from the focused corner). The Card scrolls on beneath it, and the size is kept across reloads. Hide (the chevron in its header, a tap above it on a phone, or "Hide chat" under the Feedback) folds it into a pill at the same corner, floating over the page too, with the message count or "Replying…"; a tap brings the sheet back as it was. Under the Feedback the button reads "Show chat" with the count once the chat has started.

States: graded, chat-empty, chat, chat-streaming, chat-failed, chat-cut-off, chat-long, chat-hidden

Routes: `/decks/5eed0000-0000-4000-8000-000000000002/study` (Calculus 2, write-and-grade). The plain load is the Queue's first Card: write an answer and Check, and the stubbed Grader grades it after about 2 s; then ask away, and a canned reply streams in (Stop ends it). The state pill at the bottom steps through the states: `graded` is the ln(1 + x) sketch Card, the `chat-*` states the Maclaurin series Card, as in BAN-40's `graded-mistakes`. `chat-hidden` is `chat` with the sheet hidden (it shows the pill). Nothing reaches OpenRouter.

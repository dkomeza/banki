# b: Mounted

Bet: every Image part is a card like the table's, on lines of its own, and they all share one footprint, so an upload becomes its picture, or its error, right where it stood.

- Picture: mounted on a bordered card, up to 18 rem tall, with a foot naming it ("PNG image") and "Open full size" at the foot's end on hover or focus (always on touch).
- Uploading: a muted card of the same size, spinner, the file's name, "Uploading", and an accent bar running along its bottom.
- Error: the same card tinted red: ⚠, the file's name, the message, × in its corner.
- Missing: the card dashed, empty, "Missing image" and the file name.
- Drop cursor: the side gets a dashed accent outline, and a dashed one-line slot, "Drop to add here", opens where the files will go.
- Toolbar: the Image button ends the pill after a separator; on a phone it is pinned at the ink bar's end.
- Table cell: a small thumbnail on a white mount, 2.5 rem tall.

States: picture, source, uploading, failed, missing, dragging, table, toolbar

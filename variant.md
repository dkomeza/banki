# b: Toolbar in the filter bar

Bet: selection is a mode of the list's own sticky bar: a toolbar heads it while selecting, so the count and actions stay in view at the top with the search and Tag chips still usable under them, and each Card's check takes the place of its time in the margin, like a mail client.

- Enter: **Select cards** beside the Cards heading. Leave: Cancel in the toolbar, Esc, or any Move or Delete.
- Toolbar: Cancel, "3 selected" with "1 hidden by the filter" under it, Select all 4 (the shown count), Move… (accent), Delete.
- Picker: the Move deck sheet as a searchable combobox (docs/design.md: pickers are searchable comboboxes): Folders as plain headings; typing narrows to a flat list with each Deck's path; arrows and Enter pick.
- The Move is faked in the browser (no API yet); Delete is real.

States: selecting, selected, picker, picker-one, moved

# c: Checklist and panel

Bet: selecting is a different, denser view: each Card folds to one line (its Front) with a check at its end, so a long Deck fits on a screen, and a selection panel names every selected Card, hidden ones too, so nothing a Move or Delete takes is out of sight.

- Enter: **Select** beside the search field. Leave: Done in the panel, Esc, or any Move or Delete.
- Panel: from 1024 px a sticky card in the page's right column ("3 selected · 1 hidden", the list with ✕ to unselect, "Hidden by the filter" on hidden ones, Select all 4 shown, Move…, Delete); below it docks at the foot of the screen with the list folded behind the count.
- Picker: the Move deck sheet with Folders as small-caps headings and each Deck as a taller row with its cover and card count; "Here now" on the current Deck.
- The Move is faked in the browser (no API yet); Delete is real.

States: selecting, selected, picker, picker-one, moved

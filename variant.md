# a: Ledger

Bet: decks are a list you scan, not a gallery: Home is one card of dense rows (name, first line of the description, created date), the sort a segmented Alphabetical/Newest switch above it, and create, edit and delete all happen in place, the forms opening inside the page on a muted background, with only delete's confirmation in a dialog.

States: `/`: `empty`, `create`, `create-taken`, `newest`. `/decks/$deckId`: `edit`, `delete`, `no-description`, `not-found`.

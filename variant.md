# j: Ask at the spot

A question is about a spot, so it is asked there. Under the Feedback (from 1024 px, under your answer, with the Feedback in its margin as in BAN-40), "Ask the Grader" lists the points the Grader made: each mistake with its note, each part missing from yours, and a last row, "Anything else", for the Card as a whole. Each row has "Ask about this". It opens the input right under that row, and the row's questions and replies stay there as a thread under the point, like comments in a document. Once a row has questions its button reads "Reply · <count>". It is still one chat to the Grader: each question carries the point it is about, and every reply sees all the threads. Nothing floats and nothing is a new surface: the screen keeps BAN-40's layout, and the threads only push the Back down. The long-chat advice shows above whichever input is open. While an input has the focus below 1024 px, the dock steps aside for the keyboard.

States: graded, chat-empty, chat, chat-streaming, chat-failed, chat-cut-off, chat-long

Routes: `/decks/5eed0000-0000-4000-8000-000000000002/study` (Calculus 2, write-and-grade). The plain load is the Queue's first Card: write an answer and Check, and the stubbed Grader grades it after about 2 s; then ask away, and a canned reply streams in (Stop ends it). The state pill at the bottom steps through the states: `graded` is the ln(1 + x) sketch Card (one missing point), the `chat-*` states the Maclaurin series Card, as in BAN-40's `graded-mistakes`, with the questions under Mistake 1 and Mistake 2 (`chat-long` adds some under "Anything else"). `chat-empty` opens the input under Mistake 1. Nothing reaches OpenRouter.

# e: Snap sheet

b's sheet, opened from "Ask about this" under the Feedback (a's place for it), in a few set sizes it snaps to, and hidden back into the Feedback so the page is whole again. Below 1024 px the sheet rises from the dock at low, half or tall. Drag the grip on its top edge and let go to snap to the nearest; tap the grip, or the size button in the header, to go between half and tall. The grip's dots show which size it is at. From 1024 px it is a side sheet at the window's right edge, narrow or wide (half the window). Drag its left edge and let go, or use the widen button, and the Card makes room to match. The sizes are kept across reloads. Hide (the chevron in the header, a tap above it on a phone, or "Hide chat" under the Feedback) puts the sheet away altogether. The chat folds into the Feedback where "Ask about this" was: a small dashed card with the message count, or "Replying…" while a reply streams on, the latest reply's first line, and Show chat, which brings the sheet back as it was.

States: graded, chat-empty, chat, chat-streaming, chat-failed, chat-cut-off, chat-long, chat-hidden

Routes: `/decks/5eed0000-0000-4000-8000-000000000002/study` (Calculus 2, write-and-grade). The plain load is the Queue's first Card: write an answer and Check, and the stubbed Grader grades it after about 2 s; then ask away, and a canned reply streams in (Stop ends it). The state pill at the bottom steps through the states: `graded` is the ln(1 + x) sketch Card, the `chat-*` states the Maclaurin series Card, as in BAN-40's `graded-mistakes`. `chat-hidden` is `chat` with the sheet hidden (not in the Prototype section's list; it shows the chat folded into the Feedback). Nothing reaches OpenRouter.

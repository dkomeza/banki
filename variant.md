# g: Free window

d's sheet as a window floating over the page, which the User puts wherever it covers least, so the Card never moves or narrows for it. From 640 px (tablet and desktop) it opens at the bottom right, just above the dock: drag its title bar (the dotted grip) to move it anywhere between the top bar and the dock, and any edge or corner to resize it; a double click on the title bar puts it back at the bottom right. Arrow keys move it from the focused title bar and resize it from the focused top-left corner. Its place and size are kept across reloads. Hide (the chevron in its title bar, or "Hide chat" under the Feedback) folds it down into its title bar at the window's bottom edge, with the message count or "Replying…" while a reply streams on; the folded bar moves too, and a tap on it unfolds the window as it was. Below 640 px it is d's sheet from the dock, floating with its grip, and hides into a pill at the bottom right. Under the Feedback the button reads "Show chat" with the count once the chat has started.

States: graded, chat-empty, chat, chat-streaming, chat-failed, chat-cut-off, chat-long, chat-hidden

Routes: `/decks/5eed0000-0000-4000-8000-000000000002/study` (Calculus 2, write-and-grade). The plain load is the Queue's first Card: write an answer and Check, and the stubbed Grader grades it after about 2 s; then ask away, and a canned reply streams in (Stop ends it). The state pill at the bottom steps through the states: `graded` is the ln(1 + x) sketch Card, the `chat-*` states the Maclaurin series Card, as in BAN-40's `graded-mistakes`. `chat-hidden` is `chat` with the window folded into its title bar (the pill on a phone). Nothing reaches OpenRouter.

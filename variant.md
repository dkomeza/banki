# l: Margin, notes on call

Builds on d (Margin), as the Maintainer asked, keeping d's layout closest: the mistakes are marked as in d, with no number, and the margin beside your answer stays clear until you point at one (or Tab to it); then its note appears in the margin, level with it, as d's did. The Feedback sits beside the Back, as in d, and stays in view (sticky) while a long Back, pictures and all, scrolls past. Below 1024 px there is no margin: a tap on a mistake opens its note under its paragraph, and the Feedback comes after your answer, before the Back, in the page. The dock is d's.

States: grading, grading-selected, graded, graded-mistakes, grade-failed, no-key

Routes: `/decks/5eed0000-0000-4000-8000-000000000002/study` (Calculus 2, write-and-grade). The plain load is the first Card's Front; write an answer and Check, and the stubbed Grader answers after about 2.5 s with a canned grade. The state pill at the bottom steps through the states. `graded-mistakes` shows the Maclaurin series Card, its Back now with a plot, in place of the first one (Edit opens the real one).

# a: Ledger

Bet: every Macro is a row of two fields, name and definition, always open for writing, with its render (`\norm{x} → ‖x‖`) and any error or "Replaces KaTeX's" note under it; on a Deck, its own rows and Settings' read-only list sit side by side (stacked on phones), and a Settings' Macro the Deck overrides is struck through with "Overridden here". Deck settings' Math macros is a full-width section under Desired retention.

States: empty, list, adding, invalid, builtin, save-error

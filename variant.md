# b: Result pane

Bet: seeing the result beats reading a list: the popup is a slim list of commands with a pane beside it that shows the whole Formula as it would be with the active command accepted, and the cheatsheet is a palette docked at the page's edge that stays open while you insert from it.

- Popup: one-line rows (command, its slots' shape, its render at the right), the active row filled in the accent; beside it the Result pane: the Formula being written with the active command in it, the snippet and its meaning. The pane stands in for the inline Formula's render card while the popup is open.
- Slots: soft accent pills.
- Cheatsheet: a panel docked at the right edge from 1024 px (the writing page moves over to make room); below 1024 px a sheet over the lower half of the screen, the side still in view above it and, on a phone, the ink toolbar riding on top of it. One list under sticky group headings with counts; rows of render, command (with its slots) and meaning. A row inserts and the panel stays open.

States: completing, completing-cell, slots, cheatsheet, cheatsheet-search, cheatsheet-empty

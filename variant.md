# i: Facing pages

Builds on d (Margin), with your answer and the Back facing each other instead of one above the other. From 1024 px they are two equal columns, so the comparison reads straight across, the mistakes marked on the left and the missing part on the right; below 1024 px they stack. Each mistake's note sits under the paragraph of its mistake at every size, so nothing has to be kept level in a margin. The Feedback comes after both, full width and in a readable measure, its place held from Check, so landing only lengthens the page. Missing parts are one even tint with a solid underline. In the dock, a selection before the suggestion turns its own cell into "Confirm": tap it again, or press Enter.

States: grading, grading-selected, graded, graded-mistakes, grade-failed, no-key

Routes: `/decks/5eed0000-0000-4000-8000-000000000002/study` (Calculus 2, write-and-grade). The plain load is the first Card's Front; write an answer and Check, and the stubbed Grader answers after about 2.5 s with a canned grade. The state pill at the bottom steps through the states. `graded-mistakes` shows the Maclaurin series Card in place of the first one (Edit opens the real one).

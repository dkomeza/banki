# b: Countdown

Bet: the Plan is a count of the Study days left, in large type, over a strip of them (one segment a day, ending in the Deadline's flag); the form keeps a month open in the card with the days you'd study on shaded up to the picked one, sets the goal with a stepper and a few common amounts, and the check reads as a sentence with a bar of the new cards learned in time.

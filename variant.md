# e: Slip

The Written answer comes back like a marked test: the Back first (on the left from 768 px, on top on a phone), and beside it, or under it, the answer on a paper slip the Grader marks. A stamp in its corner carries the suggested Rating (an empty dashed "Marking" stamp while it works), each mistake is circled in red with its number, the Back's missing part is run over with a highlighter, and at the slip's foot the marker's note holds the Feedback and then the numbered notes, on ruled lines while they're coming. The slip is the only thing that changes when the result lands, and nothing sits under it, so nothing moves. In the dock a fifth cell holds the Grader's action: Confirm a selection while it marks, Take the suggestion (Enter) once it's in, or Try again / Settings after a failure; the suggested rating's label is stamped too.

States: grading, grading-selected, graded, graded-mistakes, grade-failed, no-key

Routes: `/decks/5eed0000-0000-4000-8000-000000000002/study` (Calculus 2, write-and-grade). The plain load is the first Card's Front; write an answer and Check, and the stubbed Grader answers after about 2.5 s with a canned grade. `graded-mistakes` shows the Maclaurin series Card in place of the first one (Edit opens the real one).

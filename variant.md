# b: Ledger

Rows to read, not tiles to admire: a small Study card with due, new and the estimate as figures beside the pill, and a receipt-like summary (label left, value right, between hairlines) with the actions docked at the bottom like the ratings.

States: `/decks/$deckId` (plain) and `?state=nothing`; `/decks/$deckId/study` `?state=summary`, `?state=summary-more`, `?state=summary-early`, `?state=summary-new`.

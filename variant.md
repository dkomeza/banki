# a: Desk

The study screen stays inside the shell, as one more page of the Deck: one paper card in a reading column, the Front above a hairline and the Back below it, with the four ratings as a row of rounded buttons under the card and Back to the Deck and Undo in a header row above.

States: revealed, math, undone, rate-error, done, nothing

Prototype notes: the Interval previews are made up on the client (the API doesn't answer them yet), Ratings are saved for real, and Undo only steps back on screen (the Review stays saved). Formulas show a tint on hover once revealed, and a click copies them.

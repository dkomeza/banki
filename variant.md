# j: Bare board

h (Board) as the Maintainer chose it, with no chrome on the Front: no menu over it (no size, no Fold away), and no "Bring the Front here".

Built on h, from the Maintainer's round-3 feedback ("I like h. We can remove the scale & fold away buttons from the front, and the 'Bring the front here' button."). The Front is stuck on the grid paper. Tap it to select it (an accent frame and a handle at each corner, nothing else), drag anywhere on it to move it, and pull a corner to scale it about the opposite corner, from 40% to 250%. Tap the paper to let go of it. A long Front starts scaled down to fit 60% of the canvas.

The board zooms, from 30% to 300%: two fingers pinch, Ctrl+wheel zooms about the pointer, and the control at the bottom-left has − / +, the zoom (tap it for 100%), and Fit, which shows the Front and all the ink at once. Fit is how the Front is found again after panning it out of sight. One finger or the wheel pans. Keyboard: Tab to the Front, arrows move it, + / − scale it, 0 is 100%.

States: ink, ink-written, ink-long-front

Routes: `/decks/5eed0000-0000-4000-8000-000000000002/study` (Calculus 2, write-and-grade). The state pill steps through the states; Handwrite in the top bar (`?handwriting=1`) puts whatever Card shows into handwriting. The ink is rough prototype ink: a pen or a mouse writes.

# e: Live fields

Bet: built on b (Rows), there's no Edit and no form to open or close: each row's name and definition are fields that read as plain text beside its render tile, and focusing one turns the row into its form on a muted background, with the fields' outlines, the hint and a delete button; leaving the row (a click or tap elsewhere, Tab past it, or Escape) puts it back to reading. Problems and the built-in note show under a row whether it's focused or not; a new row left blank goes away. On a Deck, Settings' follow under "From Settings" with Override, as in b.

States: empty, list, adding, invalid, builtin, save-error

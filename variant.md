# b: Sheet

The typed screen's layout, with a ruled sheet for a pen: at every width the Front sits above, in the typed Front's column (up to 48 rem), and the sheet takes the rest of the screen under it. A Front taller than about a quarter of the screen folds to that height with a fade, and "Show all" on the fade opens it whole over the top of the sheet (scrolling there if it is longer still); "Show less", or the pen landing on the sheet, folds it again. The tool bar is a strip between the Front and the sheet, "Your answer" on its left as on the typed screen; on a phone the tools spread across it. The dock and the top bar are as today.

States: ink, ink-written, ink-long-front

Routes: `/decks/5eed0000-0000-4000-8000-000000000002/study` (Calculus 2, write-and-grade). The state pill steps through the states, which show the Maclaurin series Card (or a long Front) in place of the first one; Handwrite in the top bar (`?handwriting=1`) puts whatever Card shows into handwriting. The ink is rough prototype ink: a pen or a mouse writes, one finger or the wheel pans, and the tools work.

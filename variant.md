# h: Board

Miro all the way: the Front is an image on the board, and the whole board zooms, so the Front and the ink grow and shrink together.

Built on f (Sticker), from the Maintainer's round-2 feedback ("more like images on Miro"), and on g (Picture): the Front is stuck on the grid paper with no chrome of its own. Tap it to select it (an accent frame, a handle at each corner, and a menu over it with its size and Fold away), drag anywhere on it to move it, and pull a corner to scale it on the paper, about the opposite corner, from 40% to 250%. Tap the paper to let go of it. It always shows whole, so a long Front starts scaled down to fit 60% of the canvas.

What h adds: the board zooms, from 30% to 300%. Two fingers pinch it, Ctrl+wheel (a trackpad's pinch) zooms about the pointer, and the zoom control at the bottom-left has − / + , the zoom (tap it for 100%), and Fit, which shows the Front and all the ink at once. Zoomed out, you see your working and the question together; zoomed in, you write small things large. One finger or the wheel still pans. Keyboard: Tab to the Front, arrows move it, + / − scale it, 0 is 100%. Panned or zoomed out of sight, "Bring the Front here" fetches it back.

States: ink, ink-written, ink-long-front

Routes: `/decks/5eed0000-0000-4000-8000-000000000002/study` (Calculus 2, write-and-grade). The state pill steps through the states; Handwrite in the top bar (`?handwriting=1`) puts whatever Card shows into handwriting. The ink is rough prototype ink: a pen or a mouse writes.

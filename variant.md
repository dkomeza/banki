# b: Desk

Two panes from 1024 px: a compact numbered list of the file's Cards, narrowed by filter chips (all, ticked, left out, Duplicates, invalid, warnings), beside the picked Card in full, where Edit turns the pane into the editor; on phones the list opens each Card in a sheet. Both sources sit side by side on the import page, and the report is a receipt of figures beside what was skipped.

States: paste, refused, uploading (on `/decks/$deckId/import`); preview, edit, many, confirming, report-clean (on `/imports/$importId`; its plain load is the seeded report).

# b: Verdict

The Card stays one reading column after Check (the Front, Your answer, the Back), and the Grader's verdict lives in the dock with the ratings it is about: a panel of fixed height from Check (Grading… with Confirm once a rating is picked; then "Suggests Hard" and the Feedback, which More opens upward over the Card; or the failure with Try again or Settings), so nothing on the Card moves when the result lands; mistakes are underlined in red and a tap opens a note under them, the missing part is underlined in the accent, and the suggestion wears a "Suggested" tab on its button.

States: grading, grading-selected, graded, graded-mistakes, grade-failed, no-key

Routes: `/decks/5eed0000-0000-4000-8000-000000000002/study` (Calculus 2, write-and-grade). The plain load is the first Card's Front; write an answer and Check, and the stubbed Grader answers after about 2.5 s with a canned grade. `graded-mistakes` shows the Maclaurin series Card in place of the first one (Edit opens the real one), with its first note open.

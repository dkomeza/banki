# d: On the rules, with chips

Bet: a's print on the page and a's toolbar, with c's chips for what is transient, centred in the side, so an upload or an error stands out from the text without moving it.

Built on a (round 1), with c's uploading and error chips, as the Design Review verdict asked: "the toolbar from a, the loading card from c, the loader / error centred in the editor, the image preview from a".

- Picture (from a): its own size up to the side's width and 16 rem tall, a hairline border; "Open full size" is a pill on its top-right corner on hover or focus (always on touch).
- Uploading (from c, centred): an accent-tinted chip, a spinner and the file's name, with a thin bar running under it, centred in the side on the line the reference will take.
- Error (from c, centred): a red chip, "⚠ **name** · message ×", centred the same way.
- Missing (from a): a dashed tile the shape of a small print, "Missing image" and the file name.
- Drop cursor (from a): an accent rule between lines, tagged "Drop to add here" at its end.
- Toolbar (from a): the Image button ends the pill after a separator; on a phone it is pinned at the ink bar's end while the other tools scroll.
- Table cell (from a): a small framed thumbnail, 3 rem tall.

States: picture, source, uploading, failed, missing, dragging, table, toolbar

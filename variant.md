# c: Note

The canvas is the whole screen between the top bar and the dock, on grid paper, and the Front is a note pinned over its top-left corner: across the top below 1024 px, 26 rem wide from there. A long Front scrolls inside the note, up to 45% of the screen. "Fold away" folds the note to a one-line tab with its first words, and the tab opens it again; the canvas pans under it, so nothing written is lost behind it. The tool bar is a column floating at the canvas's right edge. The dock and the top bar are as today.

States: ink, ink-written, ink-long-front

Routes: `/decks/5eed0000-0000-4000-8000-000000000002/study` (Calculus 2, write-and-grade). The state pill steps through the states, which show the Maclaurin series Card (or a long Front) in place of the first one; Handwrite in the top bar (`?handwriting=1`) puts whatever Card shows into handwriting. The ink is rough prototype ink: a pen or a mouse writes, one finger or the wheel pans, and the tools work.

# b: Focus

The study screen leaves the shell and takes the whole window: plain paper with only the Card, a thin top bar (close ×, the Deck's name, Undo), and the actions docked at the bottom in thumb reach, Show answer and then four equal ratings led by their Interval in large type.

States: revealed, math, undone, rate-error, done, nothing

Prototype notes: the Interval previews are made up on the client (the API doesn't answer them yet), Ratings are saved for real, and Undo only steps back on screen (the Review stays saved). Formulas show a dashed outline on hover once revealed, and a click copies them.

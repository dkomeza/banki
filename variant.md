# b: Ink strip

The toolbar is a fixture of each side: pill-grouped buttons on top of the side's first line, faded until the side has the focus (on a phone only the focused side's, sticking to the top of the screen). The inline Formula's render is an ink chip under it, a display Formula's render an inset on muted paper captioned "Preview", and the table is ruled like the page, its header in small caps and its ⋯ in the margin beside it.

States: inline-editing, display-editing, broken, table, table-menu, toolbar

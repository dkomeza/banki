# c: Tiles

Bet: Macros are index cards like the cheatsheet's (the render first and large, the name and definition under it), with a dashed "Add a macro" tile; picking one opens its editor in a panel under the grid, with a large live render. On a Deck, one grid holds everything its Formulas can use, sorted by name: its own tiles solid, Settings' dashed (picking one offers "Override in this deck"), and its own that replace one of Settings' flagged "Replaces Settings'". Flags on a tile also mark "Not saved" and "Replaces KaTeX's". Deck settings' Math macros is a full-width section under Desired retention.

States: empty, list, adding, invalid, builtin, save-error

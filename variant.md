# c: Two halves

Bet: where a chart sits says whether the range moves it. The page has two halves under headings of their own: "Your year", the activity calendar and Cards by stage, which never follow the range; then "The last 30 days" (or 7, 90, All time) with the range as tabs on its rule, over Minutes per day, Actual retention and Grader agreement. Each card opens with a sentence that carries its figure in the accent ("You recalled 88.6% of 907 reviews of learned cards…"). On a phone the year stands up, a week a row with this week at the top, so its days stay big enough to tap. The deck page's Statistics pill sits after Export.

States: range-7, range-all, empty, no-graded, tooltip

The statistics are in memory in this Variant, about 120 days of generated Reviews up to today, as the Seed data's history Deck will hold them. The per-Deck page shows the same kind of history for whichever Deck it opens.

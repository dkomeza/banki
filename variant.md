# e: Corners

The Front is a note that lives in one of the canvas's four corners: drag it and it glides to the nearest one, and A− / A+ step its size.

Built on c, with the Maintainer's round-1 feedback: a wheel or a finger over the Front scrolls the Front, never the canvas. The note's header (the grip and "Front") drags it, by pen, mouse or finger; let go, it glides to the nearest corner (the right ones keep clear of the tool bar), so it never sits half over the middle of the writing. A− and A+ in its header step its size from 75% to 200%: the type, the Formulas and the Image grow together (CSS zoom), and where the note can't get any wider (on a phone), its text wraps instead. Ctrl+wheel (a trackpad's pinch) over it steps the size too. It stays in its corner, at its size, from Card to Card and across reloads. "Fold away" folds it to a one-line tab in its corner. The tool bar, the top bar and the dock are as in c.

States: ink, ink-written, ink-long-front

Routes: `/decks/5eed0000-0000-4000-8000-000000000002/study` (Calculus 2, write-and-grade). The state pill steps through the states; Handwrite in the top bar (`?handwriting=1`) puts whatever Card shows into handwriting. The ink is rough prototype ink: a pen or a mouse writes, one finger or the wheel pans.

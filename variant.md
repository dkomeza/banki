# a: Ledger

One column, a checklist: every Card is one numbered row (Front and Back side by side, ticked in its margin, Duplicates locked in place) that opens in place to edit, with warnings above the list, invalid Cards below it, and Confirm in a bar stuck to the foot of the screen.

States: paste, refused, uploading (on `/decks/$deckId/import`); preview, edit, many, confirming, report-clean (on `/imports/$importId`; its plain load is the seeded report).

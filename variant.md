# a: Ink dock

Bet: selection stays on the timeline itself (each Card's dot turns into a check, the whole Card toggles), and every action lives in one floating ink dock at the foot of the screen, within thumb reach on a phone.

- Enter: **Select** beside the search field. Leave: ✕ in the dock, Esc, or any Move or Delete.
- Dock: "3 selected · 1 not shown", Select all (Select none once every shown Card is), Move… (accent), Delete.
- Picker: the Move deck sheet, with Folders as small-caps headings and each Deck with its mini cover; "Here now" on the current Deck.
- The Move is faked in the browser (no API yet); Delete is real.

States: selecting, selected, picker, picker-one, moved

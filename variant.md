# c: Drawer

Bet: a Deck's settings stay out of the way of its Cards, in a "Deck settings" dialog (a sheet from the bottom on phones) opened from a pill on the deck page that shows the Deck's retention. Desired retention reads as a picture, ten cards coming due with the ones you'll remember filled in, under one picker whose first choice is Follow Settings; settings to come are a short "Coming later" list in the same dialog. Settings' Study card shows the same picture. On the deck page, every `?state=` opens the dialog.

States: follows, own, picking, save-error

# d: Free note

The Front is a note that goes anywhere over Variant c's full-screen grid canvas, and scales like a picture from its corner handle.

Built on c, with the Maintainer's round-1 feedback: a wheel or a finger over the Front scrolls the Front, never the canvas. The note's header (the grip and "Front") drags it anywhere over the canvas, by pen, mouse or finger. The round handle at its bottom-right corner scales it, from 60% to 200%: the type, the Formulas and the Image all grow and shrink together (CSS zoom), while the header and the handles keep their touch size. Ctrl+wheel (a trackpad's pinch) over it scales it too, and the percentage in its header puts it back to 100%. It stays where it was left, and at its size, from Card to Card and across reloads. "Fold away" folds it to a one-line tab where it sits. The tool bar, the top bar and the dock are as in c.

States: ink, ink-written, ink-long-front

Routes: `/decks/5eed0000-0000-4000-8000-000000000002/study` (Calculus 2, write-and-grade). The state pill steps through the states; Handwrite in the top bar (`?handwriting=1`) puts whatever Card shows into handwriting. The ink is rough prototype ink: a pen or a mouse writes, one finger or the wheel pans.

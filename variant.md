# a: Desk

The Front beside the canvas: from 768 px (a tablet in portrait, and wider) the Front is a column on the left, a third of the width up to 26 rem, and a long Front scrolls in its own pane with a "Scroll for more" fade; the canvas takes the rest, edge to edge down to the dock. On a phone the Front sits above the canvas in a band of at most a third of the screen, scrolling the same way. The tool bar floats at the top of the canvas as a pill. Faintly ruled paper. The dock and the top bar are as today.

States: ink, ink-written, ink-long-front

Routes: `/decks/5eed0000-0000-4000-8000-000000000002/study` (Calculus 2, write-and-grade). The state pill steps through the states, which show the Maclaurin series Card (or a long Front) in place of the first one; Handwrite in the top bar (`?handwriting=1`) puts whatever Card shows into handwriting. The ink is rough prototype ink: a pen or a mouse writes, one finger or the wheel pans, and the tools work.

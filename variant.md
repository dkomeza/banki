# c: Tree beside

Bet: folders are a place you navigate, like Finder: from 1024 px the whole folder tree sits beside the page (the place shown in the accent, the path to it open, every row a drop target), a place's folders are small outlined index tabs above BAN-11's paper stacks, the breadcrumb is a one-line path bar that scrolls sideways, every tile has a ⋯ menu, and Move… slides in from the right as a panel holding the tree.

States: `/`: `empty`, `new-folder`, `drag`. `/folders/$folderId`: `empty`, `rename`, `move`, `clash`, `delete`, `deep`, `not-found`. `/decks/$deckId`: `move`, `clash`.
